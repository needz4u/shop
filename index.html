Frequently Asked Questions

How does Invo stay free? ▼
We are a registered non‑profit funded entirely by community donations and grants. No investors, no profit motive — just a mission to make crypto accessible for everyone, everywhere.
Which wallets are supported? ▼
We support any wallet that uses a standard 12‑word recovery phrase, including MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet, Ledger, Trezor, Exodus, and 20+ more popular wallets.
Are there any hidden fees? ▼
Never. The only cost is the blockchain network gas fee, which goes to miners/validators — not to Invo. We charge absolutely nothing on transfers or swaps.
Can I donate to support Invo? ▼
Yes! As a non‑profit, we welcome donations. Every contribution helps maintain the platform and keep it free for the entire community.
⭐ Invo never charges fees for transfers or swaps. Only standard network gas fees apply. We are a non‑profit.
